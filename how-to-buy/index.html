How To Buy

This guide assumes you are starting from scratch. If you know what you’re doing, copy the contract address and snatch $ABC right away.



1. Download Metamask Wallet

This is a free Ethereum wallet. Download it, install it, save your private keys (which you should never share). 


2. Buy ETH

Your wallet will be empty initially. You can send yourself ETH from exchanges, but if you’re totally new you can buy it from the wallet for a small fee. You can choose your provider and go through the buying process.


3. Choose Swap

Click swap and make sure ETH is on top. Click the secondary asset (USDT) and swap that for a different token.


4. Choose ABC

Paste the contract address in the address bar and choose Armoured Barbie Chicken (ABC). On the next screen choose the amount of ETH you want to swap for it. Please leave yourself a little ETH for exchange fees (aka Gas).


5. Swap

Now you can see what you can buy for your ETH. Review the order then press Swap!
